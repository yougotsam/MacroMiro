When a trade is allowed

In the file nowA trade needs a valid setup, independent evidence, a calibrated outcome model, positive edge after costs, and final risk approval. No setup means no trade.

S4

Regime

In the file nowLabels: trend up, trend down, range, compression, expansion, shock, unknown. ADX14 under 18 is range, over 25 is trend. EMA50 on the 1-hour plus a break of structure is direction. Bollinger width and ATR mark compression, expansion, and shock.

Option, not appliedDelete the labels, or pick one and make it a yes or a no.

S5

13-point stack

In the file nowStructure up to 2. Sweep, location, fib up to 3. Volume and flow up to 2. Trend group up to 2, and that group names EMA 7, 14, 50, 200, RSI14, Stoch RSI, and MACD. Regime up to 2. News up to 1. The entry candle up to 1. Under 8 reject, 8 watch, 9–10 research, 11–13 high. At least 4 groups. The score is never a percent and never overrides price or risk.

Option, not appliedRemove the points. Or keep the list as a note that cannot send.

S8

Structure math

In the file nowA swing needs 3 bars on each side. A break of structure needs a close beyond the pivot by 0.15 ATR plus a large body. A fair-value gap needs 0.12 ATR. Fib position is defined. The file says the fib zone still has to be tested.

Option, not appliedLeave them as definitions, or turn one distance into a pass/fail on the ticket.

S9

Money math

In the file nowYES edge is the lower chance minus the YES ask minus fees. NO is the same on the other side. A maker is not a guaranteed fill. Research floors written here: maker above 1¢, taker above 4¢. The file says those floors are not validated. Never add a ±4 point chart adjustment without calibration.

Option, not appliedMake this paragraph name the same cents as config.ts, or change the cents in both files together.

S10

News

In the file nowSpark parses the event. Alexandria stores old reactions. Neither may authorize a trade. A major scheduled number blocks new trades from 30 minutes before until at least 15 minutes after. Exchange outages and oracle failures are a hard no.

Option, not appliedMake that clock a real refuse, or delete the sentence.
